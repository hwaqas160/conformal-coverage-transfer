\paragraph{Limitations (fixed).}
(i) Two datasets and one architecture family; (ii) small CPU-trained models, so absolute accuracy is below published state of the art; (iii) the observed gap
is an association---its cause (annotation, sampling, agent mix) is not isolated, only a sampling-rate confound was tested; (iv) marginal, not conditional, coverage;
(v) hypotheses added after Run~1 are labelled post-hoc; (vi) Proposition~\ref{prop:ident} covers scale shifts only.
