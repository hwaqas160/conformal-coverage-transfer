\section{Experimental Setup}\label{sec:setup}
\paragraph{Data} Argoverse~2 \cite{wilson2021argoverse2}, nuScenes \cite{caesar2020nuscenes} and Waymo Open Motion
\cite{ettinger2021waymo} (30 of 150 validation shards), converted with ScenarioNet \cite{li2023scenarionet} and loaded
through UniTraj \cite{feng2024unitraj}. AV2 scenarios are independent 11\,s clips with one focal agent; the labelled AV2
validation split is divided by a fixed salted hash into a calibration half and a test half. nuScenes has about 65 agents per
20\,s scene (9041 from 138 validation scenes), so its calibration/test split is made by scene (64/74 scenes). Waymo
scenarios carry 3.7 agents on average and are split by scenario (\wmNScenariosCalAvW\ calibration, \wmNScenariosAvW\ test).
\paragraph{Forecasters} AutoBot-Ego \cite{girgis2022autobots} with $K=6$ modes. The \emph{confirmatory} models were trained
after the hypotheses about them were registered, with UniTraj's recipe (effective batch 128, learning rate
$7.5\times10^{-4}$ halved at epochs 10--50): \textbf{av2\_gpu\_full} on the full 183k-scene AV2 training set and
\textbf{ns\_gpu\_full} on the full nuScenes training split. Each had to reach validation minADE\textsubscript{6} within
15\% of UniTraj's published value \cite{feng2024unitraj}; they reached \minADESixConf\ (gate 0.98, reference 0.85) and
\minADESixConfRev\ (gate 1.39, reference 1.21). The final model is the last checkpoint, fixed in advance. Four
\emph{reduced-budget} models (training-data subsets or shorter schedules; the three AV2 models reach minADE\textsubscript{6}
1.09--1.35) were
used to design the analyses and are labelled exploratory throughout.
\paragraph{Protocol} $\alpha=\delta=0.1$. Forward: calibrate on the AV2 calibration half; test on the AV2 test half
(same-domain control), nuScenes and Waymo. Reverse: calibrate on the nuScenes calibration scenes; test on nuScenes test
scenes, AV2 and Waymo. Label-budget experiments draw $k$ agents or $m$ whole scenes 1000 times from a labelled pool that
is disjoint from the evaluation pool. Intervals on multi-agent targets resample scenes.
\paragraph{Registration and disclosures} Hypotheses, thresholds and success criteria were written to a
version-controlled, timestamped log (released with the code) before the runs they govern; later analyses were added as dated
addenda, and outcomes are appended, never edited. Defects found during the work are disclosed there: a cache collision,
per-agent instead of per-scene nuScenes splits, and truncated scenario identifiers, none affecting a reported number; and
a betting $p$-value whose bet cap allowed negative capital. That last defect was found in an internal review before
submission, a regression test that detects it was added, and every repair result in Sec.~\ref{sec:repair-scene} was
recomputed with the corrected code under a design registered before the rerun.
