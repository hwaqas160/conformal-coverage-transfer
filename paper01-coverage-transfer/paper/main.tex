\section*{Data and Code Availability}
Argoverse~2, nuScenes and Waymo Open Motion are third-party datasets available under their own licences from
\url{https://www.argoverse.org}, \url{https://www.nuscenes.org} and \url{https://waymo.com/open}. Code, configuration
files, the registered hypotheses, the experiment log and all aggregate result files (no raw third-party data) are at
\url{https://github.com/hwaqas160/conformal-coverage-transfer} and archived under
\href{https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23054412}{doi:10.5281/zenodo.23054412} (CC~BY~4.0). Every reported result, table and
figure is regenerated from the result files by the \texttt{paper/make\_results*.py} scripts; dataset descriptions (AV2
training-set size, the 30 of 150 Waymo shards, scene length) come from the dataset documentation and our conversion logs.
